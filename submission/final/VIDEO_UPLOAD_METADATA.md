## Concise description (YouTube/Vimeo)

PauseSignal is consumer protection against voice-impersonation and
coercion scams. Two evidence engines — acoustic anomaly analysis and a
bilingual (English + Egyptian Arabic) semantic threat engine — fuse
into one understandable Threat Score that creates a pause before
action. This video demonstrates the labelled Demo Attack flow, a real
persisted incident report with SHA-256 audio provenance, Analyze
Recording, Family Shield's privacy-minimal alert design, and a genuine
RevenueCat Test Store purchase through the real `purchases_flutter`
SDK: the paywall loads live Offering prices, the native Test Store
purchase sheet completes a sandbox transaction for `sentinel_monthly`,
`CustomerInfo` flips the `sentinel` entitlement active, the UI updates
to SENTINEL ACTIVE, and Restore Purchases re-derives the same tier. No
real money is charged — it is a RevenueCat-hosted sandbox transaction.
Open source (MIT): https://github.com/NemesisDevX/voxguard
