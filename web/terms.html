PauseSignal Terms of Service

Last updated: September 21, 2026

What PauseSignal is

PauseSignal is an assistive consumer safety and risk-awareness tool. During protection sessions you start yourself, it produces a composite Threat Score from acoustic anomaly indicators and conversation-risk signals, explains the evidence, and can help you alert family members you have configured.

What PauseSignal is not

  • Not an emergency service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number. PauseSignal does not contact authorities.
  • Not a law-enforcement or legal-evidence authority. Output is AI-generated heuristic telemetry, not forensic or judicial determination.
  • Not a guaranteed fraud prevention tool. A low Threat Score does not prove a call is safe; a high score does not prove a call is a scam. Always verify suspicious requests through an independent, trusted channel — e.g., call the person back on a number you already trust.
  • Not a scientifically validated deepfake detector. Acoustic anomaly indicators are an assistive heuristic prototype.
  • Not a call interceptor. PauseSignal analyzes only audio from sessions you start or files you explicitly choose; it cannot access native cellular calls.

Family Shield

  • Family Shield sends an alert to your configured Trusted Circle only when you explicitly trigger it. "Accepted" means the relay accepted the request — not confirmed delivery to any device.
  • Responses (Safe / Still Suspicious) are human judgments by your contacts. PauseSignal relays them; it does not verify them.

Subscriptions

  • Paid plans (Sentinel Shield, Family Vault) unlock additional capabilities described in the app. Billing is handled by your app store via RevenueCat; cancellation, refunds, and subscription management follow the store's terms.
  • Demo Store environments simulate checkout and never charge money.

Acceptable use

Do not rely on PauseSignal as your only defense, do not use it to harass or surveil others, and do not present its output as legal evidence of a crime.
